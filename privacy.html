Privacy Policy

Mini Soccer Brawl
Effective Date: May 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mini Soccer Brawl handles information when you use the game.

1. Information We May Collect

Mini Soccer Brawl is an offline arcade game. We do not require you to create an account. Depending on your device settings, platform permissions, and integrated third-party services, the game may process limited data such as:

  • Device identifiers, including advertising identifiers where permission is granted
  • Basic app and device information, such as model, operating system, language, and app version
  • Gameplay progress stored locally on your device
  • Advertising and attribution data related to ad delivery, impressions, clicks, and reward events
  • Security or fraud-prevention signals used to protect the app and related services

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and improve the game
  • Save your local progress and settings
  • Deliver ads and measure ad performance
  • Support attribution, analytics, fraud prevention, and app security
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Advertising and Tracking

The game may use third-party advertising SDKs. On supported Apple devices, tracking-related access may depend on the App Tracking Transparency permission shown by iOS. If you deny permission, certain identifiers may not be available for personalized advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

Mini Soccer Brawl may rely on third-party service providers for advertising, analytics, attribution, and security functions. Those providers may process data according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

5. Data Storage

Your gameplay progress, settings, and similar local preferences are generally stored on your device. Some operational or advertising-related data may be transmitted to third-party services when relevant features are used.

6. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through an account system. If you believe information has been collected in a way that raises a privacy concern, please contact the developer through the contact options made available in the App Store listing.

7. Your Choices

You may be able to limit or manage data use by:

  • Reviewing your device privacy settings
  • Managing tracking permissions in iOS settings
  • Resetting or limiting ad tracking identifiers through Apple device settings where available
  • Stopping use of the app

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date when appropriate.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions about Mini Soccer Brawl, please use the developer contact information associated with the App Store listing for this app.

About the Game

Mini Soccer Brawl is built for short, satisfying soccer battles. Move, jump, and kick your way through a lively single-player campaign while facing increasingly tricky AI rivals. Matches are quick, controls are easy, and every goal feels rewarding.

Highlights

50 Campaign Levels Progress through a growing challenge curve with more pressure and stronger opponents.
Simple Controls Move left, move right, jump, and kick. Easy to learn, fun to master.
Offline Play Enjoy the full game without creating an account or relying on online matches.
Cartoon Soccer Style Bright stadium scenes, playful players, and clean arcade presentation.
